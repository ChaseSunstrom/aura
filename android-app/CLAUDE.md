# android-app — the phone and the car

Loaded when you work under `android-app/`; the root `CLAUDE.md` still applies.

- **Build here:** `export JAVA_HOME=$HOME/.local/jdk ANDROID_HOME=$HOME/Android/Sdk PATH=$HOME/.local/jdk/bin:$HOME/.local/gradle/bin:$PATH`, then from `android-app/`: `gradle :app:assembleDebug :app:testDebugUnitTest --no-daemon -q`. 1–3 min and a 3 GB heap: one gradle at a time beside a pytest run, in the background with a log. One class: `--tests "*Hermes*"`.
- **Goldens:** `make goldens` (repo root) re-records the phone goldens (the console's pictures are `make sweep`); one class: `:app:recordRoborazziDebug --tests "*Name*"`. Commit the PNGs under `app/src/test/screenshots/`.
- **The Kotlin's mirror:** `make test-android` runs `tools/*.py`, the executable spec of the Kotlin; judge it by its exit code. Logic in both languages is bound by a shared table in `tests/contracts/*.json`, never a "keep in step" comment: change the Kotlin, change its mirror, run it.
- **The phone's home is the console:** `MainActivity` hosts the console HUD in a WebView (`ui/ConsolePage.kt`, the `JarvisPhone` bridge); settings stay native. When the console cannot load, the phone and the car show the last console they loaded, from the web view's cache (`ConsolePage.showCached`, T-0711), and with nothing cached `app/src/main/assets/console-offline.html`, generated from the console's `/offline` route by `jarvis-web/scripts/offline-asset.mjs` (CI fails a stale copy).
- **The car** (ADR-0047): the app is also the phone side of wireless Android Auto, folded in from Headway. `car/` holds the JVM protocol stack and head-unit emulator (`:car-protocol`, `:car-transport`, `:car-dash`, `:car-voice`, `:car-emulator`, and the Android `:car-video`, `:car-input`, `:car-audio`); `ai.jarvis.app.car.*` the link service, the car screen and its tabs. Only the car's own socket is bound to the car's Wi-Fi, so Jarvis keeps its adapter link for the whole drive. Headway's ADRs, protocol notes and blockers are `docs/car/`. Each drive's field log is an adapter artifact ("car log headway-*").
