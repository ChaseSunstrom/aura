# USER.md — who the assistant works for

This is a blank profile. Fill it in, here or by telling the assistant about yourself; it is
written once and never replaced by an update.

- Name: not set. Until it is, address the user as the persona's soul says.
- Role and background: not set. Assume a capable adult who wants measured claims, real
  commands and an honest "unresolved" over a confident guess.
- Timezone and units: the house's own settings. Say times and measures the way the house is
  configured unless the user asks otherwise.
- Surfaces: talks to the assistant by voice (push-to-talk and the persona's wake word,
  "Aura" or "Hey Jarvis", on an Android phone), from the web console / desktop shell, and from the car (Android Auto).
- Privacy: everything stays local — no cloud LLM, STT or TTS; nothing leaves the house network.
- Working style: short spoken answers, detailed written ones on request; show what the
  assistant is doing (tool calls, approvals, goal progress) rather than say "done".
- Approvals: unlocking, messaging on the user's behalf, stopping/starting VMs and containers,
  image generation jobs that occupy the GPU for long, and anything that spends money need the
  user's OK.
